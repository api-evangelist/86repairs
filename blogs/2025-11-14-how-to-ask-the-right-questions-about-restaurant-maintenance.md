Asking the right questions is an important first step in getting ahead of any challenge. Albert Einstein is quoted saying, "If I had an hour to solve a problem, I would spend the first 55 minutes determining the proper questions to ask. For once I know the proper question, I can solve the problem in less than five minutes." For restaurant operators, the challenge of repairs and maintenance (R&M) is unending.
